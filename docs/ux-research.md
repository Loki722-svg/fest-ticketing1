# UX Research (Design Thinking)  — UX lead fills this in

## 1. Empathize
Interview at least 5–8 students. Questions:
- How do you currently find out about fest events?
- How do you register and pay today? What is annoying about it?
- Have you ever missed an event or lost a ticket? What happened?
